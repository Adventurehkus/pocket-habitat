# Pocket Habitat — Game Design Document

## Overview
**Pocket Habitat** is a polished, low-poly Roblox habitat builder where players collect resources, plant and grow flora, discover and nurture unique creatures, complete engaging quests, and upgrade their personal floating island habitat.

## Core Gameplay Loops
1. **Collection Loop**: Explore the habitat island to gather raw materials such as Wood, Stone, Dewdrops, and Seeds.
2. **Growth Loop**: Plant seeds in garden plots, water and tend to them over time, and harvest mature plants and crops.
3. **Discovery Loop**: Maintain specific habitat conditions (flora density, water sources, decoration) to attract and discover rare low-poly creatures.
4. **Progression Loop**: Complete daily and story quests, earn currency (Bells and Shards), unlock new island sectors, and purchase aesthetic or functional habitat upgrades.

## Player Experience & Tone
- **Tone**: Cozy, relaxing, welcoming, and rewarding.
- **Pacing**: Designed for both short casual play sessions and rewarding long-term progression.
- **Controls**: Fully optimized for mouse & keyboard (PC), gamepad (Console), and touch screen (Mobile).
